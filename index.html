THE MODEL

Transparent by design

Every score change is visible. No black box and no claim of medical validation.

30

Sleep

Largest possible deduction because short sleep meaningfully changes the training decision.

28

Fatigue

User-reported energy is weighted across five levels, from fresh to exhausted.

24

Soreness

Muscular discomfort changes intensity and can shift a session toward recovery.

16

Stress

High life stress reduces the capacity available for demanding training.

8

Recent load

A hard session yesterday adds a transparent recovery penalty.

Prototype boundary

The weights are an explainable decision-support model for this hackathon prototype. They are not a diagnostic, injury-prediction, or medically validated scoring system.
